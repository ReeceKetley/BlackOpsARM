# Tablet capture — 1 November 2026

BlackOpsARM **0.2.0-dev**, HONOR Pad X8a / Snapdragon 680 / Adreno 610.
The engine renders at 960×540 with SGSR upscaling and a 30 FPS cap. PNGs are
1920×1200 device screenshots; videos are 1280×800 Android screen recordings,
encoded as H.264 at 30 frames/s for playback. No audio track was recorded.

- [Menu to gameplay, 85 seconds](menu-to-gameplay.mp4): the beginning of one
  continuous device recording, from the title/menu through actual map loading
  to in-game movement/firing. Trailing idle footage was trimmed; loading was
  not accelerated and there are no internal cuts.
- [Kino combat, 50 seconds](kino-combat.mp4): Ray Gun firing against real
  round-one zombies, movement/reload and a Thundergun/theatre view. Trailing idle
  footage was trimmed; there are no internal cuts.

Developer god mode, weapon grants and teleports were used for this showcase,
including during recording. This is not an unassisted run or an additional
save-system acceptance test. Kills and points in the combat clip come from
normal shots and game callbacks. No original user save was replaced.

The in-game overlay reports the live render submission rate, CPU timings and
process RSS. GPU timestamps are unavailable in these captures and are shown
honestly as unavailable. Recording adds overhead; these are examples rather
than sustained performance benchmarks. Brief existing engine warnings can
appear during startup. Screenshots and recordings have not been retouched.
